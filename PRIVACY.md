# Privacy Policy: Edge Alt-Tab Extension

**Effective Date:** March 18, 2026

## Overview
"Edge Alt-Tab" is a browser extension designed to provide a "Most Recently Used" (MRU) tab switching experience. We value your privacy and aim to be transparent about how we handle data.

## Data Collection and Usage
*   **No Personal Data Collection:** This extension does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or identifiable user data to external servers.
*   **Local Storage Only:** The extension uses `chrome.storage.local` to store the order of your open tabs (using internal browser Tab IDs) so the "Alt-Tab" behavior remains accurate across browser restarts. This data is stored strictly on your local machine and never leaves your device.
*   **Permissions Disclosure:** 
    *   **Tabs:** Used solely to track the active state, title, and favicon of your open tabs for the switcher UI.
    *   **Storage:** Used to persist the tab order locally.
    *   **Scripting:** Used to inject the visual overlay onto web pages for the tab switching menu.

## Third-Party Services
The extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools. Your data is not shared with any third parties.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the extension's store listing.

## Contact
If you have any questions or feedback, please contact the developer via the support channel in the extension store.
