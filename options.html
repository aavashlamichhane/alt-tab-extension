EDGE ALT-TAB

Your tabs, a shortcut away.

Hold your shortcut modifier, cycle through recent tabs, then release it to switch. Use the toolbar for search and pages that cannot show an overlay.

Switching

Enter and Escape also work in the switcher. Search uses Enter to confirm. Internal Edge and Chrome pages use a toolbar popup. If the modifier was released before it opened, press Enter or click a tab.

All windows mode also focuses the selected tab's window. Normal and private tabs are kept separate.

Troubleshooting

After trying a shortcut, check whether Edge delivered it to this extension. This status contains no tab titles or addresses.
